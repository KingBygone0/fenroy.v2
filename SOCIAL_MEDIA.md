# Fenroy Shop — Social Media Branding Guide

## About Fenroy
Fenroy is a supermarket based in **Kumasi, Ghana** with a physical market location and an online store at **fenroy.shop** — serving customers in-store and delivering across Ghana.

---

## Recommended Primary Username

**`@fenroyshop`**

> `@fenroy.shop` won't work on most platforms — Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook, and YouTube all prohibit dots in usernames. Stick with `@fenroyshop` across all channels for consistency.

---

## Username Options (ranked)

| Username | Availability Tip | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `@fenroyshop` | Check all platforms | Best — clean, consistent everywhere |

## Brand Voice

| Tone | Description |
|---|---|
| Friendly | Speak like a trusted neighbourhood market, not a corporation |
| Community-rooted | Kumasi-proud — reference local life, culture, and events |
| Confident | Own your products and service — no wishy-washy language |
| Direct | Clear CTAs — visit us, shop online, order today, link in bio |

## Checklist Before Going Live

- [ ] Upload matching banner/cover image on Facebook & X
- [ ] Add `fenroy.shop` link to all bios
- [ ] Add Kumasi address to Facebook and Google Business
- [ ] Set up WhatsApp Business with the store number
- [ ] Enable Instagram Shopping (link to product catalog)
- [ ] Pin an intro/welcome post on Facebook & X
- [ ] Post first content: a photo/video of the physical market to establish credibility
